Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Personal Health Export is operated by yydleo for personal use with the operator's own Google account.

Data accessed and purpose

With authorization, the application accesses exercise, steps, sleep and sleep stages, heart-rate variability, oxygen saturation, resting heart rate and respiratory rate through the Google Health API. This data is used for personal backups and descriptive summaries. Drive file access is used to create and maintain application-created summary documents. OAuth credentials and tokens are used to maintain authorized access.

Processing and storage

The configured Google Cloud Run job retrieves a rolling 35-day window by default. Raw records and run manifests are saved in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket; a summary is saved in Google Drive. Credentials are stored in Google Secret Manager. Runtime logs contain operational status and record counts and may contain the summary document URL. The configured compute and bucket region is europe-west1; this does not imply all Google services, including Drive and Secret Manager, are restricted to that region.

Sharing and external services

Google services process and store the data to provide this functionality. The application does not sell health data, use it for advertising or send it to an AI API. The operator may separately choose to share records or summaries with ChatGPT or another service; that action is outside this exporter's automatic workflow and is governed by the chosen service's terms and privacy practices.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Each run creates a new archive. Archives remain until the operator deletes them; the 35-day download window is not an automatic deletion period. The latest summary is overwritten on subsequent runs, while Google Drive may retain revision history. Secret Manager may retain previous credential versions.

To stop collection, disable the Cloud Scheduler job and revoke the application's access in Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete existing copies. To remove stored data, delete the relevant Cloud Storage objects and Google Drive document, empty Drive trash where appropriate, and destroy stored secret versions. Provider retention and backup policies may still apply.

Public website and contact

This static website has no login, health-data upload form, analytics scripts or application cookies. GitHub Pages, as hosting provider, may process technical request information under its own privacy policy. Do not post health records or credentials in a public GitHub repository or issue.

For questions, use the operator's support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen. This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
