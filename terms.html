Terms of use

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Personal Health Export is an independently operated personal backup and summary tool. It is intended for use with the operator's own accounts and authorized data.

Limitations

Availability and completeness depend on the wearable, synchronization and Google APIs. Missing data is unknown and must not be interpreted as a normal reading. Summaries are descriptive and are not medical advice or official Google or Fitbit Premium scores.

Operation

The operator is responsible for account access, cloud costs and the retention and deletion of stored data. Collection can be stopped by disabling the scheduled job and revoking authorization. Existing stored data must be deleted separately as described in the privacy policy.

This application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Fitbit. Support is available through the operator's support email shown on the OAuth consent screen.
